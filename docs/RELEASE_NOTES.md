- Mission music plays at the war-table volume in every mission, also when you join a mission in progress or deploy from elsewhere on the Super Destroyer, where it was 6 dB louder.
- Mission music no longer goes silent on swamp, desert, oasis, deciduous and coniferous forest, hive world and active rift planets; they now play the music's default version, the one grassland planets play.
- The drop music on those planets plays in full instead of being cut short.
- The volume is set once when a mission starts; the planet fix is a change to the game's music sound bank and runs no code.
- Measured in the game: 0.005 ms per frame on the ship.

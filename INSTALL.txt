Sound Systems Rebalanced v1.0
Supports Helldivers 2 Steam build 25480438 / EXE 1.8.46015.0.

Install Bingus Shared Loader v18 or newer and import this ZIP into Arsenal or
HD2MM. Enable and deploy both, then restart Helldivers 2.

What it changes: every mission plays its music at the volume you hear after
deploying from the war table. Before, the music was 6 dB louder when you
deployed from anywhere else on the Super Destroyer, most often when joining a
mission already in progress. The ship's own music is unchanged.

It also stops mission music from going silent on swamp, deciduous and
coniferous forest, desert, oasis, hive world and active rift planets: those
now play the music's default version, the one grassland planets play.

On any other game build the volume fix does nothing and says so in the log:
%LOCALAPPDATA%/CowboyBingus/Helldivers2/Logs/SoundSystemsRebalanced.log
The silent-music fix is a copy of a game sound bank and cannot switch itself
off: after a game update, disable this mod until a matching version is out.

To remove it, disable or remove it in the mod manager.

https://github.com/CowboyBingus/BingusSharedLoader/releases/latest

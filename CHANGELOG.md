# v1.0

- Every mission plays its music at the war-table volume, also when you join a mission in progress or deploy from elsewhere on the Super Destroyer, where it was 6 dB louder.
- Mission music no longer goes silent on swamp, deciduous and coniferous forest, desert, oasis, hive world and active rift planets; they now play the music's default version, the one grassland planets play.
- The drop music on those planets plays in full instead of being cut short.

# Mission Music Fix v1.0

- Every mission plays its music at the war-table level, also when you join a mission in progress or deploy from elsewhere on the Super Destroyer, where it was 6 dB louder.
- The war-table room is set once when a mission starts; back on the ship its own rooms take over again as you walk between them.
